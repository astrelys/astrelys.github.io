Overview

This paper studies an explicit five-dimensional polynomial map over ℚ(√−2), with Jacobian determinant one and geometric degree four. A squarefree quartic describes a fiber of four distinct points. The paper identifies a nonproperness component whose total Hodge–Deligne polynomial is (uv)⁴, while its reduced singular locus has polynomial (uv)³ − 2uv + 1.

Dimension-normalized, componentwise singular Hodge polynomials are invariant under the equivalence generated by polynomial left–right changes and genuine identity extensions. The nonzero constant term excludes representatives of dimension below five in this equivalence class.

Condensed summary. See the manuscript for the full abstract, statements, and proofs.

Scope & limitations

The dimensional obstruction uses the specified equivalence relation. This is not a claim to the first counterexample to the Jacobian conjecture or a classification under broader notions of stabilization.

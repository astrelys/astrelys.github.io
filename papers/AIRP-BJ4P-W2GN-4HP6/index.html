Overview

For online cube-vector balancing using a global sign and coordinate permutation, the optimal deterministic prefix bound in dimension four is 5/2. In dimension three the manuscript proves 2197/1004 <= O3 <= 11/5, and identifies 11/5 as the optimum within a specified symmetric convex invariant class. It also solves a two-stream randomized subgame. The unrestricted three-dimensional value, general randomized game and offline problem are not solved.

Condensed summary. See the manuscript for the full abstract, statements, and proofs.

Scope & limitations

The unrestricted three-dimensional value, general randomized game and offline problem are not solved. Analytic continuous-input proofs are supplemented by a printed 32-state exact lower certificate and a portable integer checker. Not externally peer reviewed.

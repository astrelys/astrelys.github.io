Overview

For normalized real stable polynomials of degree n in n variables, with n ≥ 3, the power of the prescribed linear form uniquely minimizes the squarefree coefficient whenever the gradient lies within ℓ₁-distance 10⁻⁹n⁻⁸ of the all-ones vector. A quantitative coefficient gap controls root variance; further estimates control the full coefficient vector and extend to positive integer coefficient targets.

The paper also determines the exact local stability boundary. In dimension three, a commuting-minimum theorem gives sharp global Euclidean and ℓ₁ radii, their boundary minimizers, and a symmetric-marginal escape barrier whose attainment changes at the balanced marginal.

Condensed summary. See the manuscript for the full abstract, statements, and proofs.

Version 3.1.1 adds the hypothesis f(0) > 0 to Lemma 3.5 and makes the positivity used in its proof and application explicit. The main theorem statements and constants are unchanged.

Scope & limitations

The general-dimensional radius is sufficient, not optimal. Sharp global radii concern the cubic case; the full global minimization region remains open.

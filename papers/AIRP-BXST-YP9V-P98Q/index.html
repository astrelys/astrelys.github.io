Overview

This note constructs a rational four-parameter family of 48-product schemes for 4 × 4 matrix multiplication. Four polynomial trace invariants have Jacobian determinant 1/384 at an explicit rational base point, certifying four independent directions modulo ordinary isotropy.

The family cannot be covered by isotropy saturations of finitely many rational curves, even after summand permutations. A second polynomial certificate separates a distinguished curve from a specified labelled comparison-family orbit closure. Rational coefficient tables and exact-arithmetic checkers verify all 4,096 tensor identities.

Condensed summary. See the manuscript for the full abstract, statements, and proofs.

Scope & limitations

The result concerns the geometry of 48-product schemes. It neither reduces the product count nor classifies all such schemes.

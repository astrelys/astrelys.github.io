Overview

Within a specified affine-product ansatz for the normalized mixed middle-branch equation, this paper classifies polynomial solutions over a field of characteristic zero. For every integer d ≥ 1, solvability forces two coefficient polynomials to have degree at most two, and the remaining solutions have explicit cubic form.

The associated four-dimensional sweeps have geometric degree 2d + 2, certified by a univariate generic-fiber polynomial. Over the complex numbers, their critical image has dimension three, excluding polynomial left–right equivalence to the specified triangular suspension class at every even geometric degree at least four.

Condensed summary. See the manuscript for the full abstract, statements, and proofs.

Scope & limitations

The classification is restricted to the stated ansatz. These sweeps are not asserted to be Keller maps; their Jacobian has a squared transverse factor.

Overview

A complete two-parameter Lorentzian classification is established for a specified factorial-normalized symmetric polynomial family in every degree at least six and every finite variable count. The proof reduces derivative Hessians to four occupancy classes and one controlling matrix. It yields a sharp weighted rank-two Giambelli criterion and rational parameters realizing any prescribed variable cutoff simultaneously in all admissible degrees. The general Giambelli-immanant conjecture remains open; variable cutoffs in other families were already known.

Condensed summary. See the manuscript for the full abstract, statements, and proofs.

Scope & limitations

The general Giambelli-immanant conjecture remains open; variable cutoffs in other families were already known. Complete proofs are accompanied by exact symbolic identities and rational Hessian checks. Not externally peer reviewed.

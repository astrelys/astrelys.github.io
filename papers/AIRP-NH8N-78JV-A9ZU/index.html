Overview

For a prescribed positive spectrum, this paper compares the largest possible best-subset Nyström trace error with its volume-sampling mean. It determines the sharp comparison factor in dimension four, gives an exact formula for a family with two repeated eigenvalues, and characterizes equality for two-point precision spectra through equiangular tight frames.

A planar selection inequality gives a sharp regularized formula in codimension two when the dimension is divisible by three. Its limiting consequence forces any dimension-independent comparison constant to be at least 3/2. Further results include a 5/3 comparison in these codimension-two dimensions and a Plücker-based bound for the inverse energy of a best Parseval coordinate basis.

Condensed summary. See the manuscript for the full abstract, statements, and proofs.

Scope & limitations

The sharp statements apply to the specified dimensions and spectral families. The unrestricted sharp comparison remains unresolved.
